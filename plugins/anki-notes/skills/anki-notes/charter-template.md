## Scope
What this deck teaches, in one or two sentences.

## Out of scope
What looks related but doesn't get a card here.

## Adding
Automatic (whenever Claude teaches something in scope) or Ask (propose cards and wait for a yes).

## Stages
`01 <name>` → `02 <name>` → ... Numbered subdecks, introduced in order.

## Conventions
Card styles and rules specific to this subject (e.g. cloze sentences for a language), and the tag prefix: `<prefix>::<area>::<topic>`.
